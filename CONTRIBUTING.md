# Contributing to BAO Court

Thanks for your interest. To keep the dual-license model (AGPL public +
private grants) legally sound, **every contribution must be made under a
CLA** assigning copyright to baocommunity.

1. Before your first pull request, sign the baocommunity Contributor
   License Agreement (contact the repository owner for the form).
2. Ensure your contribution is your own work and contains no third-party
   code without compatible provenance.
3. Keep license headers and the NOTICE intact.

Un-CLA'd contributions cannot be accepted — the copyright of all merged
code is held by baocommunity.
